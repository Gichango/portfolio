My path in technology

My interest in technology developed through my Bachelor of Science in Information Technology at Mount Kenya University, where I built a foundation in computer systems, networking and software development. I then gained practical workplace experience through ICT support and network-related internships at the Ministry of Tourism and Wildlife, East African Breweries Limited and Wananchi Group.

Those experiences helped me understand the importance of reliable IT support, clear documentation and careful troubleshooting. Alongside that work, earning my CCNA strengthened my networking foundation and cybersecurity training introduced me to security monitoring and analysis. I am continuing to develop in these areas through Cisco's Cybersecurity Defense Analyst Career Path and personal projects. I am particularly interested in IT support, networking/NOC and entry-level blue-team opportunities.
